Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

DayHaul is a trip-planning app for professional truck drivers, operated by Absolute Media Group LLC ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and, if you choose to sign in with Facebook, your name and profile identifier from Facebook.
  • Driver profile: truck and trailer details, fuel and food preferences, and planning preferences you enter (for example, your hours-of-service cycle type and cruise speed).
  • Trip plans: the start points, stops, appointment windows, and hours figures you enter to build a plan, and the plans the app generates.
  • Approximate route data: the addresses you enter are sent to mapping and weather providers (below) to compute routes and forecasts. We do not track your live location in the background.
  • Feedback: if you send a message through the app's Feedback & support screen, we store that message so we can respond and improve the app.

How we use it

To create your plans, show route-timed weather, save your preferences, and operate and improve the app. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your trip data for advertising.

Advertising

Some screens in the app show ads. Personalized ads are off by default; they are used only if you turn them on in the app.

Service providers

  • Google Maps Platform (routes, places, geocoding) — receives the addresses and route points needed to compute your route.
  • National Weather Service (weather.gov) — receives route coordinates and times to return forecasts; no account information is sent.
  • Supabase — hosts our database and sign-in systems, where your account, profile, and plans are stored.
  • Resend — sends account emails (confirmation and password-reset messages) on our behalf.

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • You can use the app with an email account instead of Facebook sign-in at any time.
  • In the app's Profile screen you can download a copy of your data at any time.
  • In the app's Profile screen you can delete your account and its data — your account, profile, plans, briefings, and related records are erased. You can also email info@dayhaul.ai from your account email if you cannot sign in.
  • You can change or clear your profile and preferences in the app at any time.

Data retention and security

We keep account and plan data while your account is active. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access controls. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

DayHaul is for licensed professional drivers and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy at this address and update the date above.
